## CorrectionLib boundaries

The common dispatch candidates across small and large R include `JetCalibrationAlg` and its ordered `JetCalibrationTool` steps, the EtaJES class configured with distinct response data, and `JetUncertaintiesAlg` with its shared config parsing, group evaluation, histogram primitives, validity, and resolution dispatch. Large R adds JMS in MC nominal calibration and a separate FF mass-smearing algorithm. Small R adds area and residual subtraction, GSC, truth inputs and NNJvt selection and efficiency.
